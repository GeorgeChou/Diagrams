Competitors at the substrate step

  • Sumitomo Electric (Japan): the other ≈80% leader, outside China's controls
  • JX Advanced Metals (Japan): ¥120B to expand 7–10× by FY2030
  • Coherent: captive 6-inch InP, ≈4× the output of 3-inch at ≈½ cost
  • Freiberger (Germany, private)

Why the stock swings

  • Permits: late-2025 Q4 guide cut on China export delays
  • Dilution: April 2026 offering raised $632.5M, ≈28% more shares
  • Buyer diversification: customers want non-China supply (Sumitomo, JX, in-house)
  • Tongmei STAR IPO awaiting Chinese approval since 2022
